If a step is blocked and a fix hasn't worked within 1–2 minutes, stop working on it. Say in plain words what is blocked and
exactly what the person needs to do, then wait for them to finish before carrying on.

- Examples: an app won't close or quit, a pop-up is waiting for a click, a program stops answering, a tool keeps failing the same way.
- Don't leave long waits running in the hope it clears, and don't try harder ways round it.
- Work that doesn't depend on the blocked step can continue meanwhile; say that it is.
- Tools should fail as text, not pop-ups: the person can't always be there to click OK.

**Why:** long silent waits on a stuck step cost hours, while the person could have fixed it in seconds.
