For every shot with facts on screen, check each date, quote, attribution and piece of filler text against sources
while building, not afterwards.

- Before sending a shot, look up each on-screen date and claim and keep the sources.
- Put the verdicts in the reply, with links.
- Flag any mismatch with the script and offer the verified wording. Never silently change the script.
- Never put words in quotation marks unless the person actually said them; quotes are often misattributed or
  paraphrased. Offer the real wording instead.
- Record the verified facts and their sources in the project's notes so later shots can reuse them.
- When the full script is available, read the lines around the one being animated: what comes before and after can
  change the shot.

**Why:** a wrong date or a made-up quote on screen is the most expensive kind of mistake to ship.

**How to apply:** read pages word for word with the Fetch MCP (see "Reading web pages" in CLAUDE.md), not a summary.
