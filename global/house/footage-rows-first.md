A row in a video's breakdown can ask for found footage or archival pictures as well as an animation or a data graphic:
the type is FF, the ADD. column says FF, or the row's notes say "ADD FF: …" or name footage, photos, documents or
headlines. That footage is part of the beat, not something to fill in later in the edit.

1. **Before the first still,** read the row's type, its ADD. column and its notes for footage.
2. **If it asks for footage, start sourcing it at once,** alongside the idea stills, and say so: Public Domain, CC0 or
   GODL-India only, licence read on the source page, largest file found ([[approve-images-first]]). Text-only finds
   (headlines, quotes) are checked word for word on their own page and their licence stated honestly.
3. **Post the options as a numbered sheet** in the MarkRoom with its licence table, and wait for the picks.
4. **Plan the stills around the picks:** where each one sits, and how the drawing hands over to it or carries it.
5. **If it isn't clear** whether the footage goes inside the animation or beside it in the edit, ask in the first
   message about that animation, with a suggested answer.

**Why:** a row marked for footage was read as "added later in the edit", the storyboard was drawn and reviewed without
it, and the pictures then had to be found after the plan was set. Footage found first shapes the idea; footage found
last gets squeezed in.

**How to apply:** this runs before [[ask-before-building]]'s still previews; facts in the footage are checked as in
[[verify-facts-on-screen]].
